## Caveats, stated plainly
- **One run, one feature, one judge family.** The judge is a Claude model scoring Claude output against a rubric the standards were written from, so it favours the standards' structure. A human read is needed (see below).
- **Length is a confound.** The suite's plan is nearly three times as long. Length alone can lift scores, and whether it is *better to build from* is a human judgement.
- The hand-off was taken from the Gate 6 run of EV-PLAN-01, so this covers hand-off to plan only, not a full end-to-end run.
- The weakest criterion is `decisions` (3): the plan largely restates decisions from the hand-off instead of showing alternatives again.
