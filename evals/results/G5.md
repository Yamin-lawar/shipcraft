## What the runs found and fixed in the skill
1. No opening line, no first-run welcome: made the opening line a literal first message.
2. The draft was saved only at the end, so resume would not work: now a decisions log is appended after every answer (check `TC-PROGRESS` added).
3. A git email appeared as the PRD author: the skill now never takes the Developer's name or email from the system.
4. "Say now, later or no with your answer" was clumsy: suggestions are now options inside the question prompt.
5. The problem section lacked evidence and why now: the skill now always asks both, even for a side project.
6. The ending asked "All fine?" and then said "Saved": assumptions are now confirmed through the question prompt, then the closing line.
7. Open questions were written as questions, blurring the one-question rule: now plain statements.
8. Two checks were wrong, not the skill: `TC-HIST` demanded a copy for every small append (now once per rewrite), and the runner missed shell writes (now counted).
