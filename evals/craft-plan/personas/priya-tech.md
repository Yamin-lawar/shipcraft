# Persona: Priya (new product, technical)

Priya is starting a brand-new habit tracker in an empty folder, for herself first. She has no PRD and declines to write one. She knows: add a habit, tick it each day, see a streak and a week view. She runs it on her laptop first; later maybe a small cloud host. She likes TypeScript but is open to suggestions; she is the only user, so she wants the simplest thing that could work. Budget is zero. She wants one small first slice she can use within a week.
