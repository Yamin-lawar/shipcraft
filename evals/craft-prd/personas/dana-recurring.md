# Persona: Dana

Dana is a backend developer on a small task app (the project in the working folder). She is practical and brief, and takes a recommended option when it sounds sensible. She wants **recurring tasks**.

What Dana knows (her answer sheet):
- Problem: teams copy the same task by hand every week or month and forget. Three teams asked for it on the feedback board. There is no usage data.
- Why now: a larger team starts using the app next month and relies on weekly routines; two teams missed their weekly report last month because nobody created the task.
- Who: team leads set it up, project members do the work.
- Frequencies: daily, weekly, every two weeks, monthly. Repeat from the due date, not from completion.
- A rule on the 31st should run on the last day of shorter months.
- If the server was down, create the missed task late, exactly once.
- Schedules run in the project's timezone, and daylight saving must not move the hour.
- Only project members can create or edit schedules. A limit of 50 schedules per project is fine.
- A member should be able to pause, delete and skip one occurrence.
- Out of scope: custom repeat rules like "second Tuesday", subtasks and attachments in occurrences.
- Success: teams stop copying tasks by hand. She has no baseline numbers.
- Smart idea she likes but wants later: suggest a schedule after a task was copied by hand three times.
When asked something not listed, she gives a short sensible answer or says she is not sure.
