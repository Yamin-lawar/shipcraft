# Persona: Priya

Priya is a developer starting a brand-new side project in an empty folder: a simple habit tracker web app for herself. She is friendly and decisive.

What Priya knows:
- Problem: she forgets daily habits and existing apps are cluttered with social features and ads.
- Evidence: she has tried three habit apps in the last year and dropped each within two weeks, every time because of clutter or nagging notifications. That is her own experience, no survey.
- Why now: she starts a new job next month with irregular hours and wants a routine to hold on to.
- Who: just her, at first. Maybe friends later.
- Core: add a habit, tick it off each day, see a streak. A week view.
- Not now: social features, notifications, accounts for other people.
- Success: she uses it every day for a month.
- She has no data and no deadline. She wants it to run on her laptop first.
When asked about something she has not thought about, she answers briefly and sensibly.
